# FSI: Foundational Stability Indicators

## FSI-A (Adaptability)

FSI-A measures guided correctability by progressively revealing portions of a verified reference solution and estimating how rapidly the model’s continuation approaches that solution. 

## FSI-R (Retention)
FSI-R measures reformulation stability by comparing retention of solution-level alignment with retention of problem-level meaning across reformulated instances. 

## Phase Space Beyond Final Answer
Together, the indicators form a two-dimensional behavioral map that separates responsiveness to valid guidance from sensitivity to problem presentation.
